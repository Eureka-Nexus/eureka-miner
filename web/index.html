Help

Official 1.1.0 setup and safety.

Quick start

  1. The Miner connects automatically to the official public Eureka Nexus pool.
  2. Enter only your public BSC wallet address.
  3. Confirm BSC and Pool indicators are green.
  4. Mining depends on the Official Server gate. KAWPOW and RandomX are handled by the official Miner.
  5. After settlements exist, load your Merkle claim proof from the Claims page.

Never do this

  • Never paste a private key or seed phrase into the Miner.
  • Never download a Miner from an unverified third-party link.
  • Never assume reported hashrate creates EKNX; only server-validated work counts.
  • Always verify the EKNX contract on BscScan.
